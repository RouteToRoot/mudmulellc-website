What Sets Us Apart

01

Local to Southern California

We're based in Los Angeles — not Maryland or Virginia. Contracting officers in the LA Basin, Inland Empire, San Diego, and Ventura County are working with a vendor who is on the ground, not 2,700 miles away.

02

Federal Compliance Background

Mud Mule LLC operated as a federally regulated motor carrier under DOT and FMCSA requirements beginning in 2016. We don't need to learn what federal compliance means — we ran it as a core business function for years.

Owner Answers the Phone

Every contract is managed personally by the owner-operator. No account managers, no escalation chains. Contracting officers deal directly with the person responsible for performance on every order.

Logistics Is What We Do

Our commercial trucking background gives us a delivery mindset that pure supply vendors don't have — freight coordination, load specifications, last-mile delivery, and timeline accountability built in from day one.
